A gift without the wrapping. tiny.gifts folds a message and a gift code into a small card that arrives as a link — a package icon, a few lines of text, one button. That card is the whole delivery: no account to make, no app to install, nothing on either end but a browser.

The sender fills in the front. The recipient opens the link and the card is already there.

JavaScript. Live at tiny.gifts since 2021.
